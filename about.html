Square Art started as a single framing crew with a reputation for walls that were actually plumb and schedules that actually held. Homeowners kept asking one question — “can you just do the rest too?” — so we did. Trade by trade, we grew into a full residential construction company: concrete, roofing, drywall, paint, floors and finishing, all held to a framer's tolerance.

The crew — same faces, every stage
What we stand on

Three non-negotiables.

— 01

Square Means Square

We measure, check and re-check. If it isn't plumb, level and square, it gets fixed before we move on. No exceptions, no “close enough.”

— 02

Safety Every Lift

Written fall-protection plans, daily toolbox talks, WCB coverage on every worker. Everyone goes home with all ten fingers.

— 03

Schedules That Hold

Families plan moves around our dates. We show up when we say, staff the job properly, and flag weather or supply shifts early.
