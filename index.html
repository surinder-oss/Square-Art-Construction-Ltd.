From blueprint to move-in day
01

Foundation

Excavation, forms, and a 3,200 PSI pour — verified, snapped and bolted before anything rises. Square starts here.

02

Floor System

Engineered joists at 16" centres. Glued, screwed, squeak-tested subfloor — a deck you could dance on.

03

Wall Framing

Walls framed flat, squared to the millimetre, then lifted. Double headers over every opening.

04

Second Storey

Rim, joists, deck, walls — the sequence repeats, the tolerances don't loosen.

05

Roof Structure

Trusses craned, braced and blocked to the sealed layout. Flag on the ridge.

06

Roofing & Envelope

Shingles, fascia, siding and corner boards — the house gets its skin, warrantied tight against hail and chinooks.

07

Drywall, Paint & Finish

Board, tape, level-4 finish, paint, floors, casing and doors. Walkthrough with a level in hand — then keys.
