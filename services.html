Common questions

Straight answers.

What kinds of projects do you take on?
Homes, exclusively. Single-family houses, duplexes and infills, townhomes, additions, renovations, basement developments and garages. Staying residential by choice is why our crews know these details cold.
Can I hire you for just one trade?
Yes. Plenty of builders bring us in for framing only, or a homeowner hires us just for drywall and paint. You can take one trade or all nine — the standard doesn't change either way.
How long does a full home build take?
A typical single-family home runs 8–11 months from excavation to keys, depending on size, site and permit timelines. You get a written schedule with your quote and weekly updates against it.
Do you work with homeowners directly, or only builders?
Both. About half our work is directly with homeowners building or renovating their own house; the rest is with local builders who need a reliable trade partner across their lots.
What does the warranty cover?
Two years on our workmanship, in writing, on top of manufacturer warranties for materials we supply. If something we built moves, cracks or fails, we come back and fix it.
How do you handle changes mid-project?
Every change goes on paper with a price before anyone swings a hammer. No surprise invoices at the end — you approve the cost and the schedule impact first.
